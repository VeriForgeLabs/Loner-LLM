Copy this template into a new campaign chat after installing the core package. Fill only what you already know. Leave uncertain details open for play or explicitly delegate them to the game master.

Goal, Motive, and Nemesis may emerge from the setting and opening scenes. Their absence does not by itself prevent fair play.

## What normally blocks play

Ask for clarification only when the missing information prevents one of these:

- understanding who the protagonist is;
- knowing whether the protagonist can plausibly attempt likely opening actions;
- establishing the immediate situation or meaningful stakes;
- respecting a content or consent boundary.

A setting detail, campaign mystery, complete backstory, Goal, Motive, or Nemesis may remain open when play can establish it naturally.

## Adding a setting later

A setting addendum may define lore, capabilities, prohibitions, genre conventions, and tone. It should not restate the generic Loner mechanics unless it deliberately overrides them.

When a setting addendum is added during an existing game:

1. Preserve already established campaign facts unless the user explicitly retcons them.
2. Apply the addendum prospectively.
3. Add concise capability scopes where the setting makes a previously vague tag ambiguous.
4. Record approved fictional corrections in the ledger and configuration changes in the play profile.

## Design basis

Loner 3e defines Goal, Motive, and Nemesis as elements that should emerge organically from setting, tone, and early play, and explicitly permits beginning before they are settled. It also requires Skills to be specific enough to change how the protagonist interacts with the world. See **Loner 3e Core Rules, pp. 6-9**.
